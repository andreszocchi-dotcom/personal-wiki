### From Berkeley Haas Resume Standards
- Chronological is the default and suits single or adjacent pivots; functional groups bullets by skill for big pivots or unfamiliar industries. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#6. Formatting / structure choices|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 6. Formatting / structure choices]])
- For Andrés, chronological is correct: Deloitte to Haas to ServiceNow PM reads as an adjacent pivot. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#Functional format|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › Functional format]])
- Add an italic one-line descriptor under lesser-known organizations; his resumes already do this for Fundación Juanfe but not for Deloitte or ServiceNow. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#Company one-line descriptors|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › Company one-line descriptors]])
- Never inflate titles, dates or scope: background checks verify them, and touching a product is not the same as being its Product Manager. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#Cautionary tales (avoid)|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › Cautionary tales (avoid)]])
