# Resume Bullet Anatomy

The Haas model for a resume bullet: WHAT you did, HOW you did it, the IMPACT, and WHY it mattered. Three of the four is great; forcing all four makes bullets bloated.

## Details

<!-- source:berkeley-haas-resume-standards start -->
### From Berkeley Haas Resume Standards
- WHAT is the thing you did or made; HOW is the capability or method; IMPACT is the result; WHY is the context or problem being solved. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#1. Bullet anatomy WHAT / HOW / IMPACT / WHY|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 1. Bullet anatomy: WHAT / HOW / IMPACT / WHY]])
- Three out of four is great; not every bullet needs all four. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#1. Bullet anatomy WHAT / HOW / IMPACT / WHY|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 1. Bullet anatomy: WHAT / HOW / IMPACT / WHY]])
- In the retail-modeling example, the strongest version combines WHAT+HOW+IMPACT, leading with an action verb and ending with a quantified result (+25% sales per square foot). ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#1. Bullet anatomy WHAT / HOW / IMPACT / WHY|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 1. Bullet anatomy: WHAT / HOW / IMPACT / WHY]])
