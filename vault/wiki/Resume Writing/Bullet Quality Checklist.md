# Bullet Quality Checklist

The checks every bullet goes through before it ships: the Average/Good/Great ladder, a read-aloud test, and a quality checklist that includes Andrés's own house rules.

## Details

<!-- source:berkeley-haas-resume-standards start -->
### From Berkeley Haas Resume Standards
- Average states a responsibility; Good adds ownership and vague impact; Great adds a specific mechanism and quantified impact. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#3. Average / Good / Great ladder|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 3. Average / Good / Great ladder]])
- Read the bullet aloud; if you stumble or a listener can't parse it on one read, it is too dense. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#4. Readability / conciseness test|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 4. Readability / conciseness test]])
- House rule (2026-08-24): no em dash as a rhetorical connector inside a sentence, because it reads as AI-generated; hyphens in dates and ranges are fine. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#5. Quality control checklist (run on every bullet before it ships)|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 5. Quality control checklist (run on every bullet before it ships)]])
- Other checks: no litotes; everything literally true; at least two of WHAT/HOW/IMPACT/WHY; cut adjectives and adverbs; watch jargon and acronyms for the audience; have someone else read it back. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#5. Quality control checklist (run on every bullet before it ships)|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 5.
