# Resume Tailoring Levers

Tailoring is more than keywords: select which experiences to show, order bullets by relevance, and shift emphasis. The real audience is a human reader, not the ATS.

## Details

<!-- source:berkeley-haas-resume-standards start -->
### From Berkeley Haas Resume Standards
- The three levers are selecting experiences, ordering bullets, and emphasis (reweighting WHAT/HOW/WHY/IMPACT). ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#8. Beyond keywords — the real levers of tailoring|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 8. Beyond keywords — the real levers of tailoring]])
- The goal is to make it easy for a recruiter or hiring manager to find what they are looking for. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#8. Beyond keywords — the real levers of tailoring|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 8. Beyond keywords — the real levers of tailoring]])
- An ATS stores and ingests resumes rather than auto-rejecting them, so optimize for the human first and keywords second. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#7. ATS reality check|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards › 7. ATS reality check]])
