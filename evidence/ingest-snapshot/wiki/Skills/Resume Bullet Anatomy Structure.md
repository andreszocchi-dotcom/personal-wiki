# Resume Bullet Anatomy Structure

Effective resume bullets should detail what was done, how it was achieved, the resulting impact, and the context or reason why it mattered. Not all four elements are necessary, but including them strengthens the bullet.

- WHY is the context: why did the WHAT matter, or what problem was being solved. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#1. Bullet anatomy  WHAT   HOW   IMPACT   WHY|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md › 1. Bullet anatomy: WHAT / HOW / IMPACT / WHY]])
- The strongest bullet format is WHAT+HOW+IMPACT, which leads with an action verb and ends with quantified impact. ([[raw/Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md#1. Bullet anatomy  WHAT   HOW   IMPACT   WHY|Berkeley Haas Resume Standards.md › 1. Bullet anatomy: WHAT / HOW / IMPACT / WHY]])
